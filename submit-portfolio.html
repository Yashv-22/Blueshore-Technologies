Roster FAQ

How does project matching work?

expand_more

When an enterprise client starts an engagement, our directors query the roster database based on required tech stack and rates. Profiles that align are contacted immediately to gauge active bandwidth.

Is there an exclusive commitment required?

expand_more

No, you have full professional freedom. Roster members are completely autonomous contractors and can accept other roles concurrently, provided they hit agreed project milestones.

How are rates negotiated?

expand_more

You submit your target hourly rate expectations on registration. While rate negotiations occur per-project based on scope, we strive to meet your target expectations directly with clients.

Do I need to live in a specific country?

expand_more

No. Our network is entirely global. We embrace geographical diversity and work asynchronously across timezones, though occasional client-facing overlaps are coordinated in advance.
