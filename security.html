Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Security Questions

How can enterprise customers request our SOC 2 Type II audit report? expand_more

Enterprise clients under NDA can request our latest SOC 2 Type II report, penetration test summaries, and ISO 27001 certificate directly by contacting their dedicated Account Executive or emailing security@blueshoretech.com.

Where is client data stored and what data residency options exist? expand_more

We support localized data residency across US-East (Virginia), EU-Central (Frankfurt), and Asia-Pacific (Mumbai/Singapore) cloud regions. Data never leaves your designated geographic region without explicit written consent.

How often are database backups performed and tested? expand_more

We maintain point-in-time recovery (PITR) with automated continuous write-ahead logging and daily immutable encrypted cross-region snapshots. Disaster recovery restoration exercises are tested quarterly.

Are employee background checks required for all technical staff? expand_more

Yes. All Blueshore Technologies engineers undergo comprehensive background verification, sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and complete mandatory bi-annual security awareness training.
